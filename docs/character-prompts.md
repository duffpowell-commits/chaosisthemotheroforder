# Character prompts (Grok)

Built on the locked templates in `style-guide.md` §9, tuned for Grok Imagine.
Mara and Kesh had no look before this file; their designs here are proposals.

## Style anchor
Approved Nov 13: `assets/characters/source/you_front_anchor.jpg` (You, front, A-pose,
plain tee). Attach it to every other character prompt.

## How to run them in Grok
1. **Make You first.** Generate, pick the best of the batch. That image is the
   style anchor for the whole cast.
2. **Every other character:** attach the anchor image and start the prompt with
   "Same art style, line weight, shading and lighting as the attached image,
   but a different character:". This keeps the cast looking like one artist.
3. Portrait/tall aspect (2:3). Grok likes adding scenery; the "isolated on flat
   light grey" line is there to stop it. If it still adds a background, reply
   "remove the background, keep everything else".
4. **Back view:** attach the chosen front image and say "Same character, same
   style, turned around: three-quarter back view, same pose."
5. Save the full-size image (not a screenshot) and drop it in the chat for the cut.

Hands stay empty on purpose: the cutout rig draws weapons in code.

## Shared style block (end of every sprite prompt)
Painterly HD-2D game character sprite, clean dark outline, soft cel shading,
light from the upper left, muted grounded colours with one strong accent colour.
Full body, three-quarter front view, standing, arms held slightly away from the
body, legs slightly apart, feet visible, empty hands. Isolated on a plain flat
light grey background, no ground shadow, no text, no scenery.

## You (Duff, default protagonist)
Man in his early 30s, average build, fair freckled skin, blue-grey eyes,
reddish-blond stubble. Strawberry-blond hair, long swept top with shaved
undercut sides. Stretched earlobes with round plugs, spiked gold and black helix
rings. Tattoos: a wolf with a blue rose on the neck, geometric mandala blackwork
full sleeve on the left arm, illustrative tattoos on the right arm. Black band
t-shirt, dark jeans, worn black boots, a faded red bandana tied loosely around
the neck. Tired, steady expression. [+ shared style block]

## Teodor (dock foreman, 50s)
Black man in his mid 50s, broad and heavy-shouldered, close-cropped grey hair,
short grey beard, deep lines around the eyes. Heavy canvas work jacket over a
faded hi-vis vest gone grey with grime, work trousers, steel-toe boots, a knit
cap, a red rag tucked in his back pocket. A cigarette behind one ear. Unimpressed,
watchful expression. [+ shared style block]

## Mara (proposed: the cell's medic and fixer, late 30s)
Woman in her late 30s, lean, olive skin, dark hair pulled back in a practical
low bun with loose strands, a small scar through one eyebrow. Dark green field
jacket with rolled sleeves, grey hoodie under it, cargo trousers, worn trainers,
a canvas shoulder bag with a hand-stitched cross, a red armband on the left arm.
Calm, tired, kind but hard eyes. [+ shared style block]

## Kesh (proposed: student, lookout and hacker, early 20s)
Young man in his early 20s, wiry and restless, warm brown skin, messy black
curls under a beanie, thin moustache he's trying to grow. Oversized black
bomber jacket over a printed student-union t-shirt, slim jeans, beat-up high-top
sneakers, wired earbuds hanging around his neck, a red scarf pulled down from
his face. Alert, suspicious expression, shoulders slightly hunched. [+ shared style block]

## Regime riot guard (one sheet covers every guard)
Riot police officer, tall and bulky, full dark blue-grey riot armour
(#1A1F2B), full-face helmet with the visor down and a cold blue glint on the
visor, no skin or face visible at all, padded shoulders and shin guards, a
small number patch on the chest and no other insignia. Anonymous, rigid,
upright stance. Cold blue-grey only, no red anywhere. [+ shared style block]

## Portraits (dialogue)
Attach that character's sprite as the reference, then:
Chest-up portrait of the attached character, three-quarter view, looking just
off camera, [ONE EMOTION]. Painterly oil style, visible brush strokes, dramatic
single light source from the side, deep shadow on the far side of the face,
desaturated night street behind. Documentary mood, not glamour. No text.
Make a cold, grey version (regime district) and a warm, amber version (liberated).
