# Prop bible (present-day setting)

The setting is an ordinary present-day American/Western city. "Regime" means
normal policing and surveillance; "liberated" means mutual aid and open doors.
Use this list in every scene prompt. Character looks live in
`docs/art/identity/`, technique in `docs/art/style-anchor.md`.

## Approved props

- **Housing:** two- and three-storey garden apartment complexes (brick and
  siding, exterior stairs and walkways, balconies), cinderblock side walls,
  parking lots, dumpsters, chain-link fences, interior hallways with steel doors.
- **Rail:** freight yards with intermodal container cars, plain diesel
  locomotives, floodlight masts, signal masts, steel pedestrian footbridges,
  burn barrels for pickets.
- **Street:** gas stations with plain canopies, convenience stores, power-line
  poles, streetlights, traffic lights, traffic cameras, dome security cameras,
  ordinary sedans and pickups.
- **Police:** ordinary police cruisers and SUVs (light bars drawn dark or
  unlit), sawhorse barriers, flashlights, police interview room (steel table,
  plastic chair, one-way window). Officers use the existing riot-guard design
  unchanged (`docs/art/identity/guard.md`).
- **Media:** smartphones (blank screens, or a face with a black bar), laptops
  and TVs with plain lids/bezels, cable-news studio desk, phone livestream frame
  (record dot, hearts, blank comment bubbles).
- **Movement:** red ribbon bows on poles and wrists, a strip of red cloth on a
  fence, red armbands, wheat-paste flyers, mutual-aid folding tables, bikes,
  sidewalk chalk (off-white plus red only).

## Banned props

- Armoured vans, APCs or military vehicles.
- Propaganda screens or giant tele-screens; public loudspeaker towers.
- Concrete blast walls, militarized checkpoints, candy-striped barrier arms,
  torn checkpoints.
- Cult-of-personality or leader posters, portraits and billboards; flags and
  banners as set dressing.
- Soviet-style concrete blocks or brutalist housing estates.
- Drawn weapons of any kind (guns, batons, knives, tools held as weapons).
- Brand logos or badges on cars, laptops, phones, shops or clothing; readable
  text, numbers or licence plates.

## Palette notes

- Movement red `#9E1A14` is **only** for the movement (ribbons, armbands, rags,
  bandanas, flyers, chalk). Keep it small in painted scenes.
- **No maroon, burgundy, pink or orange on extras.** Civilians and crowds wear
  olive, brown, tan, grey or charcoal.
- **No coloured locomotive stripes** or rail livery; locomotives are plain dark
  grey/black. No coloured shop stripes or gas-station branding.
- Police light bars are dark (no red/blue glow), except the woodcut `res_heat`
  icon, which uses movement red by design.
- Regime-side woodcuts: paper `#E9E0CC`, ink `#1B1714` and blue-grey only.
  Movement woodcuts: paper, ink and movement red only. Never both.
